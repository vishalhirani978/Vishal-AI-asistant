# Features & Command Reference

Vishal comes packed with tools to automate your day. Below is a comprehensive list of all his features, what they do, and example commands you can use.

---

## 📇 1. Contact Book
Allows Vishal to remember people by name so you don't have to type their email addresses or phone numbers every time.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"Add John's email as john@example.com and phone as +919876543210"*
  - *"Who is in my contacts?"*
  - *"Look up Mummy's number"*
  - *"Remove John from contacts"*

---

## 📧 2. Gmail Inbox Intelligence
Allows you to send emails and read your inbox without opening a browser. Uses secure IMAP to read your mail safely.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"Send email to John about Project Update saying 'Hey, the server is ready'"* (Uses contact book name resolution!)
  - *"Do I have any unread emails?"*
  - *"Who emailed me today?"*
  - *"Search emails from boss"* or *"Find emails about project deadline"*

---

## 💬 3. WhatsApp Integration
Send WhatsApp messages and check for unread chats via automated browser sessions.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"WhatsApp Sarah that I will be late"*
  - *"Who messaged me on WhatsApp?"*
  - *"Check my WhatsApp inbox"*
  - *"Send message to +919876543210 on WhatsApp saying 'Hello'"*

---

## 🖥️ 4. Screen Awareness
Allows Vishal to take a screenshot of your screen, read code, recognize errors, and answer questions about what you are looking at.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"What am I looking at?"*
  - *"Explain this error on my screen"*
  - *"Help me with this code"*
  - *"Explain the content of this webpage"*

---

## 📅 5. Calendar Agent
Schedule meetings, inspect your day, and manage your time.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"Add a meeting tomorrow at 2 PM"*
  - *"What's on my schedule today?"*
  - *"Show me my upcoming events"*
  - *"Delete the meeting tomorrow"*

---

## 📋 6. Clipboard & Notes
Store quick notes and access clipboard history easily.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"Save a note saying 'Buy groceries tonight'"*
  - *"Search my notes for 'groceries'"*
  - *"Show my notes"*
  - *"Copy 'Hello World' to my clipboard"*
  - *"Show my clipboard history"*

---

## ⚙️ 7. System Controls
Operate your Windows machine with system-level commands.
- **Example Commands**:
  - *"Lock my PC"*
  - *"Put my computer to sleep"*
  - *"Set volume to 50"* or *"Mute my volume"*
  - *"Shutdown the computer in 15 minutes"* or *"Restart now"*

---

## 🎭 8. Cosmetics, Outfits & Marketplace
Earn **Pixel Coins** by interacting with Vishal (execute tools, ask questions) and spend them on premium cosmetics:
- **Hats**: Santa Hat, Top Hat, Detective Cap.
- **Glasses**: Sleek Shades, 3D Glasses.
- **Skins**: Cyberpunk Purple, Emerald Green, Crimson Red, Matrix Neon.
- Access the **Marketplace** via Settings to purchase and equip your items.
