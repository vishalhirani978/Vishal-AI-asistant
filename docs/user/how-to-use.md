# How to Interact with Vishal

Getting started with Vishal is simple. Here is a guide on how to interact with him using keyboard shortcuts, your mouse, and voice input.

---

## ⌨️ Global Hotkeys
You can summon Vishal from anywhere on your PC using these keyboard shortcuts:

| Hotkey | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Ctrl + Alt + V** | **Summon / Chat** | Walks Vishal to the center of your screen and opens the thought bubble so you can type. |
| **Ctrl + Alt + G** | **GitHub Summary** | Walks Vishal to the center and displays a summary card of your PRs, Issues, and Mentions. |
| **Ctrl + Alt + Space**| **Voice Dictation**| Initiates voice recording. Press once to start speaking, and once more to finish and transcribe. |

*Note: You can customize these hotkeys to your liking in the Settings panel.*

---

## 🖱️ Mouse Controls

- **Drag-and-Drop Positioning**: Click and hold Vishal to drag him anywhere on your screen. His position is automatically saved across restarts, so he will stay right where you left him.
- **Right-Click Context Menu**: Right-click on Vishal to open his quick actions menu:
  - **Chat with Vishal**: Open the thought bubble.
  - **GitHub Status**: See your GitHub notification dashboard.
  - **Snooze**: Temporarily sleep Vishal for 30 minutes or 1 hour so he won't interrupt you with notifications.
  - **Settings**: Open the settings dashboard.
  - **Quit**: Exit the application.
- **Click-Through Mode**: When Vishal is in his idle state, he is completely click-through. You can click on folders, browse web pages, and play games right through his transparent window.

---

## 🎤 Voice Input (Whisper)

1. Click the **microphone icon (🎤)** inside the thought bubble, or press **Ctrl + Alt + Space**.
2. Vishal will put on his **headphones sprite** to show he is listening.
3. Speak clearly into your microphone.
4. When finished, click the **Stop icon (⏹)** or press the hotkey again.
5. Vishal will think for a moment and transcribe your speech into the chat input, allowing you to edit it before sending, or execute it immediately!

---

## ⚙️ Settings Panel
Right-click Vishal and click **Settings**, or ask him to *"Open settings"*. The settings window is divided into:
- **General**: Choose startup options, scale size (Small, Normal, Large), and personality (Friendly, Professional, Sarcastic, Minimal).
- **Hotkeys**: Record custom key combinations.
- **Cosmetics & Marketplace**: Use your earned Pixel Coins to purchase hats, glasses, and new skins!
- **Data Viewer**: Access and edit your saved contacts, check calendar entries, view clipboard history, and manage notes.
