# Welcome to Pixel PM — Meet Vishal!

**Vishal** is not just another desktop assistant. He is a living, breathing AI desktop companion designed to be your virtual project manager, productivity booster, and interactive friend. 

Whether you need to send an email, check your WhatsApp, monitor GitHub, lock your PC, or get help writing code from what's on your screen, Vishal is right there on your desktop.

---

## 🌟 The Vision

The long-term vision of Pixel PM is to create an **intelligent desktop companion** that can:
- **Communicate**: Natural chat, voice interactions, and system alerts.
- **Observe**: Screen awareness to see what you see and help in real-time.
- **Assist & Automate**: Interact with your digital environment like Gmail, WhatsApp, and GitHub.
- **Manage**: Track your contacts, schedules, notes, and clipboard data.
- **Interact**: A gamified companion with outfits, skins, and accessories to unlock.

---

## 🚀 Key Value Propositions

1. **Always Present, Never Obstructive**
   - Vishal is rendered as a transparent, always-on-top overlay. 
   - When you aren't interacting with him, he is **click-through**, meaning you can click right through him to use your underlying apps without interruption.
2. **AI-Driven Intent Parsing**
   - You don't need to learn complex commands. Just talk to Vishal in natural English (e.g., *"Can you email mom that I will be home by 7?"* or *"What's on my schedule today?"*), and his intent engine parses what you want.
3. **Dual Voice/Text Interaction**
   - You can type your commands or use your microphone to dictate them directly using state-of-the-art voice transcription.
4. **Out-of-the-Box Security**
   - To ensure your safety, **every destructive or automated tool requires your explicit confirmation** before execution. Vishal will ask *"Yes, do it?"* or *"Cancel?"* before performing any action.
